Illegal 3-Month Security Deposit Demanded

Critical Severity • 85%

Demanding 3 months advance rent violates the statutory ceiling in Bangladesh. Under the Premises Rent Control Act, demanding or collecting more than 01 month advance rent is unlawful and non-binding.

gavel Act No. III of 1991

Premises Rent Control Act, 1991 (বাড়ি ভাড়া নিয়ন্ত্রণ আইন, ১৯৯১)

Section 10: Prohibition of Advance Exceeding One Month

Plain Language Translation Bilingual
English Summary:

"No landlord shall claim, demand, or receive any premium or advance rent exceeding 01 (one) month’s rent in connection with the grant or renewal of a tenancy."

বাংলা সারসংক্ষেপ:

"কোন বাড়িওয়ালা বা ভাড়াটিয়া চুক্তি নবায়নের সময় ০১ (এক) মাসের ভাড়ার অতিরিক্ত কোনো অগ্রিম জামানত দাবি বা গ্রহণ করতে পারবেন না। এই অতিরিক্ত অর্থ আইনত ফেরতযোগ্য।"

"10. Prohibition of receipt of premium, etc. (1) No person shall, in consideration of the grant, renewal or continuance of a tenancy of any premises, claim, demand, or receive any sum as a premium, fine, or advance exceeding one month’s rent of such premises.

(2) Any sum paid in excess of the amount permitted under sub-section (1) shall be recoverable by the tenant by whom it was paid or his legal representative."
