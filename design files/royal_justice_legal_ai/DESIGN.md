## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the authoritative, meticulous, and premium character of modern legal jurisprudence combined with state-of-the-art artificial intelligence. Built for institutional clients, enterprises, legal practitioners, and citizens navigating the Bangladeshi legal landscape, the visual voice prioritizes unassailable credibility, precision, and clarity.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** discipline with restrained **Glassmorphism** and tactile feedback:
- **Authoritative & Measured:** Deep midnight indigos and commanding cobalt hues convey structural permanence and judicial rigor, eliminating the flimsy feel of generic software.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Dense legal texts, complex clauses, and risk analyses are rendered with deliberate whitespace, distinct categorization layers, and precise typographic hierarchies.
- **Bilingual Equilibrium:** Seamless parity between English statutory drafting and Bangla script, ensuring high legibility across legal document analysis.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of institutional authority ("Royal Justice") grounded in cool slate neutrals and punctuated by functional analytical accents.

### Core Canvas & Structure
- **Primary (`#1E1B4B` - Deep Midnight Indigo):** Anchors primary actions, authoritative headers, global navigational frames, and key verdicts.
- **Secondary (`#312E81` - Royal Cobalt):** Sub-navigations, active tab highlights, and elevated structural surfaces.
- **Accent (`#2563EB` - Electric Royal Blue):** Interactive triggers, primary buttons, AI generation indicators, and focus states.
- **Page Background (`#F8FAFC` - Cool Canvas):** Provides an expansive, non-glare substrate for sustained legal document reading.
- **Card & Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** High-clarity focal cards framed with subtle cool borders (`#E2E8F0`).

### Text Roles
- **Headings (`#0F172A` - Ink Black):** Supreme contrast for statutory titles and verdict headlines.
- **Body (`#334155` - Neutral Slate):** Optimized optical density for long-form contract review.
- **Muted (`#64748B` - Slate Grey):** Metadata, statutory citations, and helper text.

### Risk & Verdict Semantics
- **High Risk / Non-Compliant:** Crimson (`#E11D48`) paired with light wash (`#FFE4E6`).
- **Fair Clause / Compliant:** Olive Emerald Green (`#15803D`) paired with light wash (`#DCFCE7`).
- **Unverified / Amber Alert:** Warm Amber (`#B45309`) paired with light wash (`#FEF3C7`).
- **Consult a Lawyer:** Midnight Purple/Indigo (`#4338CA`) paired with soft tint (`#EEF2FF`).

## Typography

The typography strategy couples clean European geometric realism with balanced non-Latin glyph rendering.

- **Primary Typeface:** `Inter` handles all structural Latin typography, UI text, numerical tables, and system displays.
- **Bangla Typeface Pair:** Apply `Noto Sans Bengali` with identical scale levels for all bilingual or native script interfaces. Line heights for Bangla must automatically inherit a +15% leading multiplier (`lineHeight * 1.15`) relative to Latin parameters to prevent conjunct glyph clipping.
- **Statutory / Contract Body:** Contract clauses must be rendered at `body-lg` or `body-md` using a minimum contrast ratio of 7:1 against card backgrounds, avoiding compressed horizontal tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

A strict **8-point geometric scale** governs spatial distribution, ensuring modular consistency across complex multi-pane review screens.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1440px viewport):** 12-column layout constrained to a maximum content container of `1200px` centered horizontally. Default gutter: `24px` (`1.5rem`), outer margin: `32px` (`2rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with fluid margin padding (`24px`). Clause inspector docks transition into overlay side-sheets.
- **Mobile (&lt; 768px):** 4-column layout with `16px` gutters and margins. Side-by-side legal clause heatmaps stack vertically into sequential analysis streams.

### Layout Mechanics
Document processing views operate on a split-pane workstation layout: 65% document view / 35% legal intelligence panel on widescreen, collapsing cleanly into stacked tabbed drawers on compact screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layering** and **soft ambient shadows** rather than harsh mechanical drops.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` flat surface.
- **Level 1 (Contract & Metric Cards):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and subtle dual-layer shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Cards & Popovers):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (AI Diagnostic Panels & Floating Overlays):** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) coupled with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur and outline `#CBD5E1`.
- **Clause Focus Ring:** Active highlighted clauses in documents utilize an inner glow `inset 0 0 0 2px #2563EB` alongside a tinted backdrop fill of `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern corporate rounded corners that soften legal severity while preserving institutional precision.

- **Cards & Primary Panels:** `12px` to `16px` (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`).
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** `8px` (`rounded-md`).
- **Chips, Badges, and Heatmap Indicators:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) or `6px` depending on data density.
- **Severity Anchor Bars:** Hard-cornered indicator bars embedded flush on the outer left boundary of flagged cards.
