The Second Party has paid BDT 135,000/- (Three Months' Rent Equivalent) as non-adjustable security deposit to the First Party.

The tenancy shall remain valid for a duration of 11 (Eleven) consecutive months commencing from 01/11/2024 and terminating on 30/09/2025.
